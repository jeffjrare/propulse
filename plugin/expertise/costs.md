# Costs

**As of:** 2026-10-29

- **Always a low–high range**, never a single point, in **Canadian dollars before tax** (say "USD" when a price is, with the
  rate used).
- **No amount without a source:** quote, measurement, dated price page, or whose estimate. Every price is **verified on
  <date>** or **unverified**; a vendor's claim is "according to the vendor".
- **Over 1 year and 3 years** (total cost of ownership), with the same structure for every option (the table in
  `templates/build-or-buy-grid.md`). On the buy side, always integrations and exit; on the build side, always operations
  and evolution.
- **By who builds:** technical partner, paid architect, classic team. Hours are ranges and the hourly rate is written;
  AI-assisted development effort rests on a measured velocity, not historical estimates.
- **Hidden costs are counted:** operations, maintenance, training, change, running both systems, compliance (impact
  assessments, annual review), exit.
- **"What is not counted":** an explicit section, always.
- **Editable assumptions:** in a calculator, every assumption is a field the client can change, and the break-even formula
  is shown.
- **Seasonality:** for a seasonal business, prefer usage pricing over flat year-round plans, and say so.
- **An amount lives in `FACTS.md`.** Two deliverables giving two ranges for the same item are a defect to fix, never two
  valid estimates.
