# Process optimisation

**As of:** 2026-10-29

**Not yet exercised on a real engagement.** General practices set for V1; correct them on the first process engagement.

- **Measure the current process before drawing the target:** steps, who does what, volumes (per day, per season), time per
  step, waiting times, errors and rework. An unknown volume is a question to the client, not a silent estimate.
- **Pain points come from the people doing the work**, ranked by frequency × cost.
- **Look for waste before tools:** re-keying, waiting, back-and-forth, needless approvals, searching for information. A
  burst of coordination calls is often better reduced by a shared resource calendar than by a calling tool.
- **Never automate a bad process.** Simplify first, then equip.
- **The target process** says what disappears, what changes hands, what the system does alone, and the estimated gain per
  step (hours, delay, errors), as a range with its source.
- **Before/after indicators:** one to three, measurable without effort, named before the change.
- **Change is a cost:** training, a period of running both, seasonality (`costs.md`).
