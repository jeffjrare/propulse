# Product and service

**As of:** 2026-10-29

- **Start from pain points, ranked by the client.** Listen, restate, have them ranked. The client's order governs the
  roadmap; a disagreement with it is said, with the reason.
- **Separate the observed need, what the client says, and the hypothesis.** An invented example is not evidence.
- **Real personas and roles:** office, field, end customer; on the end customer's side, signer, participant, observer.
- **The service blueprint:** journey stages as columns, layers as rows (what the customer lives, what the office does, the
  field, the system, external services, compliance). Filterable by milestone.
- **Each delivery is usable on its own.** No delivery that is only worth something if the next one arrives.
- **Business constraints are planning constraints** (e.g. nothing new during peak season).
- **A computed dashboard, not a typed one:** what the system can deduce from the case's state is never asked of a person.
- **Sequence rather than dates** until dates are committed. One deliverable with dates and another without is a
  contradiction to resolve, not two points of view.
- **A clickable demo to validate** when a decision hinges on "does this speak to me": playable personas, a guided tour, one
  live calculation, one edge case (a resource conflict, a change order).
- **One case seen by every role:** a single scenario walked through from each role's point of view; a design tool, not a
  review checklist.
- **Pilot and resale:** when the pilot client is also a product's first customer, every decision distinguishes "for the
  pilot" from "for resale".
