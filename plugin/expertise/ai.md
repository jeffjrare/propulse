# AI in a solution

**As of:** 2026-10-29

- **AI proposes, a person sends.** No answer, price or commitment goes out without a person's approval.
- **AI does not calculate:** quantities, prices, taxes and totals come from a deterministic calculation; AI summarises,
  sorts, transcribes, proposes.
- **Behind an AI adapter** (`architecture.md`): changing model or vendor never touches the core.
- **A named region:** a model offered in a Canadian region when personal information is involved; a model outside Canada
  is a communication outside Québec (`quebec-compliance.md`).
- **Governed by risk, not banned:** the more serious an error's effect, the stricter the human validation.
- **What is ambiguous waits for a person:** an uncertain classification goes to "To sort", never to a silent guess.
- **Usage cost counted** in `costs.md` (transcription, summaries, AI reception), from the client's volumes.
