# Template: domain analysis ("analysis and review")

One per domain that deserves a deep look (telephony, communications, estimating, fleet, identity…). A section with nothing
to say is dropped, never filled for form. Headings are translated into the dossier's language.

```markdown
# <Domain>: analysis and review
<date> · <author>

**Where things stand (<date>).** <The decision in principle or the recommendation in two or three sentences, with the
grid's result if there is one ("Grid DEC-YYYY-NNN scores … 4.05 out of 5"). What remains to settle and how (a test).>
Linked pieces: <framing sheet, grid, test protocol, decision note, visual — by ID and revision>.

## 1. Decision in principle
<What we do, why here, why not everywhere. "What this commits us to": who becomes responsible for what.>

## 2. The business's needs
| Topic | Situation | What is needed |
<Each situation cites its facts (F-NNN). Then "Answers of <date>" and "Still to clarify" (questions Q-NNN).>

## 3. Chosen architecture
<One paragraph, and the visual if there is one.>

## 4. Possible architectures
| Architecture | <what distinguishes it> | Examples verified on <date> | Verdict |
<From simplest to most integrated, with a prefix of the domain's own that is free in the dossier's naming table.>

## 5. Layer matrix
| Layer | Options | For the pilot | For later (resale, growth) |
<What we build (the glue) and what we buy or configure.>

## 6. Special cases
<What deliberately stays outside the system, and its reasonable limit.>

## 7. Market solutions, verified on <date>
<Finalists and rejects, with the reason. "Examples to shop around, not recommendations.">

## 8. Build or buy
<Effort in hours (range), 3-year cost including operations, and grid DEC-YYYY-NNN: result, gap, "what would reverse it".>

## 9. Compliance
<Law 25 and the rest, point by point: what applies, what we do, what goes to counsel. "Not legal advice.">

## 10. What we sign and what we do not
<Commitments to make now, and those to avoid while a decision is open (long contracts, porting a number…).>

## 11. Questions and next steps
<By recipient: client, counsel, vendor, us. For each, what it decides. Dated next steps.>

## Sources
<Each source dated; "verified on" or "according to the vendor".>
```
